ABOUT US
EduReach College of Engineering & Management is a premier higher education institution founded in 2005 in Hyderabad, Telangana. Spanning a 25-acre sustainable green campus in Knowledge City, Gachibowli, the institution holds NAAC A++ accreditation, NBA approval, and affiliation with Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University Hyderabad (JNTUH). With over 150 faculty members (80% holding doctoral degrees) and an enviable 15:1 student-to-faculty ratio, EduReach nurtures technical excellence, ethical leadership, and groundbreaking innovation for over 10,000 alumni thriving globally in Fortune 500 companies.

ADMISSIONS
Admission to EduReach College follows strict merit and regulatory guidelines:
1. B.Tech Admissions:
- Convener Quota (70% seats): Allotted through state government counselling based on ranks in TS EAMCET / AP EAMCET. Eligibility requires 10+2 with Physics, Mathematics, and Chemistry with a minimum of 60% aggregate. JEE Main scores are also accepted.
- Management & NRI Quota (30% seats): Direct merit-based admissions based on 10+2 aggregate marks and institutional counseling.
- Application Timeline: Applications open every year on March 1.
- Required Documentation: 10th and 12th marks memos, Transfer Certificate (TC), Conduct and Study certificates, Migration Certificate (if outside Telangana), Caste/Income certificate (if applicable), Entrance Exam Rank Card & Hall Ticket, Aadhaar card, and 6 passport-size photographs.

2. M.Tech Admissions:
- Requires a recognized B.Tech or B.E. degree in a relevant discipline with minimum 55% aggregate marks. Selection through GATE score or TS PGECET counselling.

3. MBA Admissions:
- Requires a Bachelor's degree in any discipline with minimum 50% marks (45% for reserved categories). Admission through TSICET counselling or national management entrance scores (CAT, MAT, XAT).
- Applications can be completed online at admissions.edureach.edu or in person at the Admissions Office, Knowledge City, Gachibowli, Hyderabad. Admissions helpline: +91 9876543210.
